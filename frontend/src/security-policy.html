Security Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Akshar is a static website with no accounts, no server-side data and no login, so the attack surface is small. If you find a vulnerability, please tell us privately before disclosing it publicly.

How to report

Email hello@rojanacharya.com with a description of the issue, the steps to reproduce it and its impact. We will acknowledge your report and keep you updated on the fix.

Scope

In scope: the Akshar website and its source code. Out of scope: denial-of-service testing, social engineering, and issues in third-party services or browsers.

Bounty

We do not offer a bug bounty, but we will gladly credit you if you wish.
