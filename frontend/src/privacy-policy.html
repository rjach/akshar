Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Akshar is a free typing test that runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts and no server-side database, so we do not collect, store or sell personal information.

What is stored, and where

Your settings, typing results, personal bests, saved custom texts, custom theme and any local font or background image you choose are saved in your own browser (localStorage and IndexedDB). They never leave your device and we cannot see them.

You can delete your typing history on the stats page, reset your settings in settings > danger zone, or remove everything by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Analytics, ads and cookies

Akshar uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Hosting

The site is served by our hosting provider. Like any website, the provider receives standard request information (such as your IP address and browser user agent) to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse, under its own privacy policy.

Optional third-party content

A few optional funboxes load text from third-party services only when you turn them on: the Wikipedia funbox requests articles from Wikipedia and the poetry funbox requests poems from PoetryDB. Those services receive your request as described in their own policies.
